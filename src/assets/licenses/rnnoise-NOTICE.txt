The LocalKit integration resamples to 48 kHz, processes 480-sample frames
in a Web Worker, compensates one frame of analysis delay, and optionally
mixes the denoised signal with the original. The RNNoise library is unmodified.

The public demonstration recordings are generated test signals and a
synthetic English voice, created for this project; they are not user uploads.
